# Features

MirrorShard is designed to support the entire creative workflow — from idea generation to structured writing.

---

## 🧠 Idea Processor

A non-linear, visual environment for brainstorming and idea development.

![Idea_Processor](../screenshots/features01.jpg)

### AI Features

* **AI Free Association**
  Generate three new ideas from a selected node.

* **IP Missing Link**
  Select a connection between two nodes and let AI fill the gap with a logical or narrative bridge.

* **Node Alchemy**
  Combine multiple nodes into a new, unified concept.

* **Story Archetypes**
  Build stories using structured templates such as the Hero’s Journey or Beat Sheets.

* **Template Completion**
  AI assists in expanding your story while maintaining overall structure.

---

## ✍️ Writing Environment

A minimal, distraction-free editor designed for immersive writing.

![editor](../screenshots/features02.jpg)

* **ZEN Mode & Frameless Window**
  Focus entirely on your text.

* **Spotlight Mode**
  Highlight only the current paragraph.

* **Integrated AI Writing**
  Continue writing from the cursor or fill missing parts of your text.

* **Markdown / HTML Preview**
  Real-time preview in a separate window.

* **Deep Customization**
  Customize colors, layout, transparency, and background images.

---

## 🗂️ Information Management

* **Hierarchical Outliner (Markdown-based)**
  Manage large documents with structured headings.

* **Large File Support**
  Handles very large text files efficiently.

* **SillyTavern Integration**
  Launch and interact with character simulations in a dedicated window.

---

## 📦 Export

Export your work into multiple formats.

* PDF

* DOCX (with Ruby support)

* HTML

* EPUB

* **Vertical Writing Preview**
  Supports traditional Japanese vertical text layout with ruby annotations.

---

## 🛠️ AI & Coding

* **AI Chat Integration**
  Supports Google Gemini, Groq API, and local LLMs.

* **OpenCode Integration**
  Launch coding agents inside the app.

* **Code Editor Mode**
  Syntax highlighting, AI completion, and terminal support.

---

## ⚙️ Miscellaneous

* **Atomic Save**
  Prevent data loss even during crashes.

* **Log Viewer**
  Analyze large AI logs without performance issues.
